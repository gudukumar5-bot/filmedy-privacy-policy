Filmedy Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Filmedy, a video editing application. We respect your privacy and explain below how information is handled when you use our application.

2. Information We Handle

Filmedy supports two types of account authentication:

  • Email and password: Account information, including a salted password hash, is stored locally on your device.
  • Google Sign-In: We use Firebase Authentication. Authentication information may include your Firebase user ID, name, email address and profile photo URL.

3. Photos, Videos and Projects

Your imported photos, videos, editing projects and timelines are stored locally on your device using browser storage (IndexedDB).

Filmedy does not upload your editing projects or imported media to our own project-storage server.

Exported videos are saved to your device, such as the Movies/Filmedy folder on Android.

4. Advertising

Filmedy uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. Google and its advertising services may process information such as device identifiers, IP address and advertising interactions to provide and measure advertisements.

Google's privacy information: Google Privacy Policy.

5. Firebase Authentication

Google Sign-In is provided through Firebase Authentication. Firebase processes authentication information to provide the sign-in service.

Learn more: Firebase Privacy and Security.

6. Data Storage and Security

Editing projects and imported media are stored locally on your device. Local email/password account records are also stored on the device.

We do not operate a remote database for storing your editing projects or imported media.

7. Account Deletion

You can delete your Filmedy account through the account deletion option inside the application.

Deleting an account removes its local email/password account record or deletes its Google account from Firebase Authentication.

Account deletion does not automatically delete locally stored editing projects or imported media. These files may remain on your device and may not be accessible through a newly created account.

Exported videos saved in your device's Gallery or Movies folder are not deleted when you delete your Filmedy account.

For account deletion questions, contact: gudukumar5@gmail.com.

8. Children's Privacy

Filmedy is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a service we operate, please contact us.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our application or privacy practices change. Updated versions will be published on this page.
